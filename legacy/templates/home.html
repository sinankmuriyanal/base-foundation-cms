More About

The Base Foundation is a non-governmental organization dedicated to rural development through education and social welfare situated in West Bengal, Birbhum district, West Bengal. Since its establishment, our organization has been committed to empowering rural communities, enhancing educational opportunities, and improving the overall well-being of rural residents.

At Base Foundation, we strive to address various societal challenges such as poverty, education, healthcare, and environmental sustainability. Our team of dedicated professionals and volunteers work tirelessly to implement innovative and sustainable solutions that uplift the lives of marginalized individuals and communities.

Through our collaborative efforts, we aim to create lasting change by providing education, vocational training, and skill development programs to empower individuals and enhance their employability. We also focus on improving healthcare services, ensuring access to clean water and sanitation, and promoting sustainable practices to protect the environment for future generations.

Project TeachCraft: Teachers’ Training

Enhancing teaching quality through bi-monthly training sessions focused on modern pedagogy, classroom engagement, and subject-specific development for effective educational delivery.

Guiding parents on effective child development practices, emphasizing education, care, and home-based learning to reinforce the foundation built in BASE Classrooms.

Strengthening students’ English proficiency through targeted coaching aligned with Board syllabi, improving comprehension, critical thinking, and exam performance.

05

BASE Computer Academy

Equipping senior students with essential digital literacy and advanced computer skills to thrive in academic, professional, and research-oriented environments.

79th Indian Independence Day Celebration

It marked the 79th Indian Independence Day. Base Foundation, Darul Fouz Education Centre, and Al-Fouz English Medium collaborated with Paikar Police Camp in the patriotic celebration. We presented a parade by scouts, a flower dance performance, and a special talk by the Assistant Superintendent of Police on the significance of religious unity that led the freedom struggle to the successful attainment of Independence on 15 August 1947.
